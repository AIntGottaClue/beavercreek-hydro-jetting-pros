What does grease do inside a line?

The EPA fact sheet on fats, oils and grease says grease solidifies, reduces a pipe's capacity and blocks flow. Cleaning clears what has built up. It does not replace keeping cooking oil out of the drain.

How is a grease blockage cleared?

The EPA sewer cleaning guidance describes jetting as aiming a high-pressure stream of water at the blockage or debris, with the nozzle shape chosen for the surface being cleaned. Ask which nozzle and approach suit your line.

What should Beavercreek homeowners know about the line?

Greene County provides sewer service to Beavercreek, and its sewer FAQ says that for grease, roots or obstructions the homeowner is responsible for cleaning the lateral from the house to the sewer main. It also says county regulations prohibit fats, oils and grease in the sewer system.

Questions about this drain problem

Is hydro jetting suitable for every pipe?

No. Ask a qualified professional to assess the line, access and blockage before recommending high-pressure cleaning.

Can cleaning repair a broken sewer pipe?

Cleaning removes an obstruction. It does not rebuild a damaged pipe. Ask whether an inspection shows a repair issue as well as a blockage.

What details should I give with a request?

List the affected fixtures, whether wastewater has backed up and any earlier cleaning. Mention known pipe repairs or access limits.

Is service available at my address?

Availability has to be confirmed for the address and the work. A request is not a booked appointment or a promise of service.
